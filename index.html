🎯 Calibrate test strips

Clarity defaults to AquaChek 7-Way strips. Using a different brand? Calibrate below for accurate readings.
1
Find the color reference card that came with your test strips — it's usually on the bottle label or a separate insert.
2
Take or upload photos of the card. Use 📷 to snap with your camera or 📁 to pick from your files. If it wraps around the bottle, add multiple photos (up to 4).
3
Hit Calibrate and we'll map the colors to values for your specific brand so future scans are more accurate.
